Paris is the capital of France. It is known for the Eiffel Tower and the Louvre museum.

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert sunlight into chemical energy. Chlorophyll in the leaves absorbs sunlight, which is used to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen.

Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius (212 degrees Fahrenheit) at standard atmospheric pressure. The boiling point decreases at higher altitudes due to lower air pressure.
